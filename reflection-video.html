💭 About This Reflection

This reflection video transforms the original "No Poverty" documentary into a powerful visual metaphor. The mirrored and distorted effects represent how poverty reflects across communities, generations, and societies.

Each reflection effect tells a different story:

  • Mirror Reflection: Shows the duality of wealth and poverty in our world
  • Water Reflection: Represents the ripple effect of poverty on communities
  • Kaleidoscope: Illustrates how poverty impacts multiple aspects of life
  • Symmetry: Highlights the balance we must achieve for equality
  • Tunnel Vision: Focuses attention on the core issues
  • High Contrast: Emphasizes the stark reality of economic disparity
